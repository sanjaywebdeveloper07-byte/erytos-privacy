App: Gerytos Monitor  |  Developer: Erytos Technologies  |  Last Updated: November 24, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and your rights over it.

By installing or using Gerytos Monitor you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

1.5 Notification Tokens

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send fall-alert push notifications. The FCM device token is stored in AWS and used solely to deliver alerts to your device. It is rotated by Firebase and refreshed automatically.

1.6 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Video or audio recordings
  • GPS / precise location data (location permission is required only for BLE scanning on Android — no coordinates are recorded)
  • Contacts, photos, files, or calendar data
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking identifiers
  • Children's data (the app targets adults aged 18+)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Core app functionality – authenticating your account, pairing your Gerytos radar device, displaying live vital signs and fall events on the dashboard.
  • Fall alerts & emergency notifications – sending push (FCM) and in-app alerts to caregivers when a fall is detected.
  • Device provisioning – configuring the radar device over BLE or Wi-Fi using the credentials you provide.
  • App improvement – we may review anonymised crash logs (if crash reporting is added in a future release) to fix bugs.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • All cloud data is stored in the AWS ap-south-1 (Mumbai) region.
  • Data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2+; data at rest in AWS is encrypted with AES-256.
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text — authentication is handled by AWS Cognito SRP (Secure Remote Password) protocol.
  • FCM tokens and device credentials stored locally are kept in Android's SharedPreferences in private app storage (not accessible to other apps).
  • We retain account data only as long as your account is active. Live telemetry streams (radar frames, vital signs) are not stored persistently — they are processed in real time and discarded.

6. Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Option A — Delete from within the app

  1. Open Gerytos Monitor.
  2. Go to Settings (bottom navigation bar).
  3. Tap Account > Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion. Your Cognito / Supabase account, local credentials, and device pairings are permanently removed.

Option B — Request deletion by email (for users who have uninstalled the app)

  1. Send an email to dineshkumarr@erytos.com with the subject line: Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include your registered email address in the body.
  3. We will process your request within 14 business days and confirm once your data has been deleted.

What is deleted

  • Your Cognito / Supabase user account and login credentials.
  • All locally stored caregiver preferences, resident names, and emergency contacts (on the device).
  • Device registrations in our AWS backend.
  • FCM push-notification token.

What may be retained

  • Anonymised, non-identifiable usage statistics (if collected in future releases) for app-quality improvement — these cannot be linked back to you.
  • Records required by applicable law (e.g., billing records for a maximum of 7 years per Indian IT Act and tax regulations) — only where legally mandated.
